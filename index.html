WELCOME TO THE AFTERPARTY

No musical bones
required.

  1. Meet the band. Tap any creature to hear its voice. Keys 1–6 work too, when you’re not editing a control.
  2. Make a pattern. Each row is a voice. Lit squares play as the beat travels left to right, then loops. Tap to toggle or drag to paint.
  3. Find your feel. Tempo sets the pace. Swing delays every other step for a lopsided groove. Mute a row to give someone a break.
  4. Keep your monster. Save a two-bar WAV loop with your tempo, swing, volume, and mutes. It’s made entirely in your browser.

Keyboard: Tab to the pattern, use arrow keys to move, and Enter or Space to toggle. On a small screen, switch between steps 1–8 and 9–16. Undo and redo keep experiments reversible.

Your beat stays on this device when storage is available. Playback stops when you leave the tab. Reduced motion keeps the band still. No samples, microphones, or accounts—just six original synthesized voices.
